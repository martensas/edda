\section{Introduction}

In \Regius, \textlink{Sigrdrifumal} ends abruptly at stanza 27 due to the loss of a number of pages; the so-called \textbf{Great Lacuna}.  The poetry contained on these pages undoubtedly belonged to the Walsing cycle and would have dealt further with the life of Siward and his dealings with Byrnhild and the Yivickings.

The author of \VolsungaSaga\ drew heavily from a collection of Walsing-cycle poetry closely related to \Regius.  He cites around two dozen stanzas known from \Regius\ and paraphrases many more.  Notably, however, some of the stanzas which he cites do not survive in \Regius---it is these which are edited here.  The contents of these fragments largely correspond to the part of the narrative belonging to the Great Lacuna of \Regius, and it is therefore probable that most or all of them derive from the poems once found therein.

The stanzas follow the numbering of stanzas in \VolsungaSaga.
