\section{Text}

\bvg\bva%
\alst{Ú}tan garða \hld\ hann sá \alst{u}pp of koma &
\ind \alst{þ}ursa \alst{þ}jóðar sjǫt; &
„\alst{Ú}rgar brautir \hld\ árna þú \alst{a}ptr heðan, &
\ind átt-at hér, \alst{v}erndar-\alst{v}anr, \alst{v}eru!“\eva

\bvb From without the yards he saw towering high \\
\ind the abode of the folk of Thurses. \\
“O’er the drizzling roads shouldst thou journey back hence; \\
\ind thou hast no shelter here, O defenseless man!”\evb\evg


\bvg\bva%
„Hvat’s þat \alst{f}lagða, \hld\ es stendr fyr \alst{f}or·gǫrðum &
\ind ok \alst{h}varflar of \alst{h}ę́ttan loga?“ &
„Hvers þú \alst{l}eitar, \hld\ eða hvęrs þú á \alst{l}eitum ert &
\ind eða hvat vilt þú, \alst{v}in-lauss, \alst{v}ita?“\eva

\bvb “What sort of demon is this that stands before the forecourt, \\
\ind and roams around the dangerous flame?” \\
“For whom art thou looking, or for whom art thou on the look-out, \\
\ind or what wilt thou, friendless man, know?”\evb\evg
